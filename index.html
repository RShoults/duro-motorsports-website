Good to know

Frequently asked

Do you sell dirt bikes?

No. Duro Motorsports is a repair and maintenance shop; we do not sell bikes.

How much will my repair cost?

Cost depends on the bike and the work it needs. Call the shop with your bike’s make, model, and the issue for a current estimate.

Does the request form confirm an appointment?

No. It opens a prefilled text message for you to review and send. The shop will need to confirm availability before your appointment is booked.

What should I include in my request?

Share your name, bike year and model, what needs attention, and the date you have in mind. The more detail you provide, the easier it is to follow up.
